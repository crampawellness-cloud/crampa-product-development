Crampá — Product Development

About the Project

Crampá is a Kenya-based menstrual-wellness startup developing a topical cooling gel specifically intended for menstrual-cramp care.

Project Objective

The project aims to explore a purpose-designed topical product for menstrual-cramp care, with emphasis on formulation development, product quality, usability, and appropriate safety assessment.

Current Development Stage

Crampá is in the early product-development stage. The product concept, initial formulation direction, and intended 100 ml squeeze-tube packaging have been identified.

A validated prototype and formal product-testing results are not yet available.

Development Roadmap

1. Develop an initial prototype with a qualified formulator.
2. Assess formulation properties, including pH, viscosity, stability, and packaging compatibility.
3. Conduct appropriate safety and quality assessments.
4. Establish applicable Kenyan regulatory requirements.
5. Gather structured feedback on usability and customer acceptance.
6. Assess readiness for a controlled pilot and potential commercial launch.

Planned Documentation

Open Development Note

This repository documents the product-development process. It does not contain a validated formulation, verified clinical results, or evidence of proven therapeutic efficacy. Any future results will be added when available.
